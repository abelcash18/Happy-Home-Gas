Safety First

Cooking Gas Safety Rules & Expiry Checker

Your family's safety is our top priority. Follow these 4 vital rules when using LPG cylinders at home, or test your cylinder date below.

01

Always Keep Upright

Never lay a cooking gas cylinder on its side. Liquid gas can enter the regulator and cause erratic high flames.

02

Soap Bubble Test

Never test for gas leaks with matches! Apply soapy water on the regulator nozzle and hose. If bubbles form, there is a leak.

03

Replace Hoses Regularly

Change rubber gas hoses every 2 to 3 years. Replace regulators immediately if the switch feels loose or pressure fluctuates.

04

Adequate Ventilation

Keep your cylinder in a well-ventilated space away from direct stove heat. Keep kitchen windows open when cooking.
